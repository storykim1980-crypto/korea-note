PRACTICAL ARRIVAL GUIDE · INCHEON AIRPORT → SEOUL

Choose by your hotel area, arrival time and luggage.

Rail times below are to Seoul Station—not to your hotel. We checked official AREX and Seoul City pages on 11 Oct 2026. Fares and schedules can change; confirm your flight terminal and travel date.

LOWER-FARE RAIL · ALL-STOP

AREX local train 공항철도 일반열차

To Seoul Station: about 59 min from Terminal 1 (T1) / 66 min from Terminal 2 (T2). AREX adult stored-value transit-card fare: KRW 4,750 / KRW 5,350, respectively.

Some trains may take an extra 2–6 min. A single-use transport card costs KRW 100 more; the KRW 500 deposit is refundable at your destination. Check the exact station-pair fare.
NON-STOP RAIL · RESERVED SEAT

AREX Express 공항철도 직통열차

To Seoul Station: 43 min from T1 / 51 min from T2. AREX currently lists an adult one-way fare of KRW 13,000; its page also shows normal fares of KRW 18,500 (T1) / KRW 19,100 (T2).

Buy a separate, seat-reserved ticket. Confirm the fare shown at checkout and the weekday/holiday timetable.
DIRECT TO A DESIGNATED STOP

Airport limousine bus 공항리무진

Consider it when an operator’s stop is convenient for your lodging. Seoul City estimates roughly 60–80 min, varying by route, area and traffic.

Check the operator’s current stop, departure time, fare and luggage conditions before boarding.
DOOR-TO-DOOR

Official airport taxi stand 택시 승강장

Can reduce transfers with heavy bags or several travelers. Use the signed taxi stand outside Arrivals; Seoul City describes International Taxi as offering English-, Japanese- and Chinese-speaking drivers.

Fares, tolls and journey time vary. Confirm the destination and current fare conditions before departure.

Fare-source note: Seoul City’s airport guide contains an older all-stop fare range (KRW 4,150–4,750), while AREX’s current station-pair table shows KRW 4,750 (T1 → Seoul Station) and KRW 5,350 (T2 → Seoul Station). The figures conflict. The amounts above use AREX’s adult transit-card table; recheck the operator’s fare page and purchase screen.

K-ETA exemption and the entry declaration are separate checks.

  1. 1 · K-ETA: The K-ETA notice of 23 December 2025 extends the temporary exemption for countries and regions already covered through 31 December 2026. IF KOREAN IS DIFFICULT ON 112 / 119

VISITKOREA advises saying “English please” or “Interpreter please” when the call connects. Its guide describes interpreter assistance, but does not guarantee a particular language or a connection in every case. If you can, identify your location using a nearby building or your phone’s GPS.

일본어 통역을 부탁드립니다.“Please connect me with a Japanese interpreter.” (Reference wording to show.)

If speaking is difficult, the KTO guide also points to the multilingual “Baro Emergency Reporting” app and lists Japanese among its languages. Check current app availability and device compatibility before travel; do not treat an app as a replacement for emergency calls.

Instead of restaurant rankings, start with common meal terms and questions to ask before ordering. Food and customs differ across regions, households and venues.

KOREAN TABLE / A SHORT GLOSSARY

Four words that help make sense of a meal.

Informed by the Korea Tourism Organization’s food-culture guide, published 25 July 2025. What is served depends on the setting.

한상차림

Han-sang-charim

A table arranged with several dishes alongside a meal. It is not a template for every Korean meal.

반찬

Banchan

Side dishes served with a meal. Selection and refill or extra-charge policies vary by venue.

나물

Namul

A term for dishes made with vegetables or edible plants. Ingredients and preparation vary.

쌈

Ssam

A way of wrapping a bite, often in a leaf. The leaf, filling and sauce depend on the menu.

Korean questions to use before ordering: These phrases can help you ask, but they cannot guarantee that a venue can accommodate an allergy or restriction.

이 메뉴에 뭐가 들어가요?What is in this dish?
덜 맵게 해 주세요.Please make it less spicy.
○○ 알레르기가 있어요.I have a ○○ allergy.
육수에 멸치가 들어가나요?Does the broth contain dried anchovy?

If you have an allergy, ask the venue directly about ingredients, broth, sauces and shared equipment. Do not rely on a translated phrase alone to assess safety.

Kimjang (김장), making and sharing kimchi, was inscribed on UNESCO’s Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2013. Practices vary among families and regions.

K-BEAUTY / LABEL READING

Before you buy, read the product label.

Korean cosmetics terms are used differently across brands and products. Rather than claiming a universal “best” or effect, start with the package, directions and precautions.

KOREAN COSMETICS / COMMON PRODUCT TERMS

These shelf labels are not a standard routine order.

The words below are a vocabulary aid. Products with the same category name do not necessarily share a formula or instructions.

토너

Toner

Used as a product-category name, but ingredients and directions vary. Check the specific item.

에센스

Essence

Its positioning varies by brand; the word alone does not establish a standard strength or purpose.

앰플

Ampoule

A product name or category term. Check the package for amount, frequency and instructions.

세럼

Serum

Often used for serum-type products. Read the item’s ingredient list and precautions.

크림

Cream

The category name alone cannot tell you how it will feel or whether it suits you.

선크림

Sunscreen

Check directions, the stated SPF/PA, size and precautions on the individual product.

Before you buy: confirm the exact product, size or shade, full ingredient list, directions, precautions and printed date information. Marketing claims and reviews reflect different evidence and circumstances; they are not a guarantee of effect or suitability.

Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) publishes Korean-language guidance on cosmetic labels and claims. This shopper’s note is not a complete current legal labelling checklist or import, sales or advertising advice.
