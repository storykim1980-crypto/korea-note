Travel · Shopping basics

Duty-free or tax refund? A shopper’s guide for Korea

“Duty-free” and “tax refund” can sound similar, but they are different systems with different purchase and payment steps. This guide summarizes Korea’s tourism information and flags the date-sensitive details.

Reviewed: 11 October 2026 · SEOUL NOTE

The Korea Tourism Organization’s “Comprehensive Tax Refund Guide,” dated 20 October 2025, distinguishes duty-free shopping—where tax is removed at the point of sale in designated duty-free stores—from tax refund, where eligible purchases are handled through an immediate or later refund process. A duty-free purchase is not also eligible for a second refund under the regular tax-refund process.

The basic tax-refund sequence

  1. Before paying, ask whether the shop participates in the tax-refund system.
  2. Check eligibility, eligible goods and purchase conditions; present your passport if requested.
  3. Keep the receipt and refund voucher, and confirm whether the refund is immediate or processed later.
  4. Before departure, follow the stated procedure for taking eligible goods out of Korea, including any customs check required for your case.

The process can depend on the refund method, retailer and departure point. Ask whether goods must remain unused or unopened, and where to go before checking baggage if a customs inspection may be required.

What the guide dated 20 October 2025 says

The KTO page describes eligibility for non-residents, a minimum purchase of KRW 15,000, and a requirement to take the goods out of Korea within three months. For immediate refunds, it lists a single transaction under KRW 1,000,000 and total transactions of KRW 5,000,000 or less. These figures summarize that dated page; they are not a guarantee that a traveller, item or store qualifies today.

Keep the paperwork and check before departure

At the counter, confirm whether a shop is eligible, whether passport verification is needed and which receipt or voucher you must keep. Some airport procedures may require goods to be available for inspection before check-in. The amount received can differ from the full tax embedded in a shelf price because the refund method and fees matter.

Eligibility, participating shops, thresholds and procedures may change. This article summarizes the KTO guide dated 20 October 2025; it is not tax or customs advice. Recheck the official guide, the shop’s notice and departure procedures on the date of purchase and travel.
